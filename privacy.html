Plain-language summary

Wild Cones Hockey Tournament can be played offline without creating an account. Online multiplayer is optional. When online multiplayer is used, the game processes the player name, room name, predefined phrase-chat selections and technical connection information needed to establish and maintain the multiplayer session.

The Google Play version does not provide free-form text chat. Online phrase chat is limited to predefined messages supplied by the game.

Privacy Policy

1. Who operates the game

Wild Cones Hockey Tournament is developed and operated by Daniel Janec, an independent developer based in Slovakia.

For privacy questions or requests, contact wildconeshockey@gmail.com.

2. Scope of this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the Android version distributed through Google Play and to the online services used by that version. It explains what information may be processed, why it is needed, how long it may be retained and what choices players have.

3. No account is required

The game does not require players to create an account, provide a legal name, provide an email address or sign in through a social network. Players should not include personal or sensitive information in player names or room names.

4. Offline play and local preferences

Offline gameplay is processed locally and does not require an Internet connection. The application may store limited preferences locally, such as the most recently used player name, graphics options, sound settings and control preferences. Players can remove locally stored information by clearing application storage or uninstalling the application.

5. Online multiplayer information

When online multiplayer is used, the game may process:

  • A player name selected by the player and a room name created or selected by a player.
  • The player's host or guest role, player-slot assignment and ready status.
  • Gameplay inputs and game-state information needed to synchronize the match.
  • Selections from predefined phrase-chat messages.
  • Game invitation information, including the invitation link, room name and invited player's selected name, when the invitation feature is used.
  • Temporary connection identifiers used by the signaling and multiplayer systems.
  • Technical network information required for communication, which may include IP addresses, connection timestamps, transport status, error information and server request logs.

Player names, room names and predefined chat phrases may be visible to other participants in the same online room. Public room names and basic room information may also be visible in the public-room list while the room is available.

Player names and room names containing words or expressions blocked by the game's content filter are rejected and are not accepted for use in online multiplayer.

6. Phrase chat

The Google Play version provides only predefined phrase chat. Players select messages supplied by the game and cannot enter arbitrary free-form chat messages through this feature. Phrase selections are transmitted only to participants in the current online room.

The game is not intended to operate as a social network, public discussion platform, anonymous chat service or private messaging service.

7. Game invitations and system sharing

The host of an online room may create and share a game invitation. The invitation contains a link with information required to join the room, including the room name and the invited player's selected name.

On Android, the game may open the system sharing interface so the player can choose an installed application, such as an email or messaging application, through which to send the invitation.

In supported web versions, the game may open the player's email application using a mail link or copy the invitation link to the clipboard.

The game does not access the player's contact list, select recipients, send email through the developer's server or receive confirmation that an invitation was delivered. Recipient details entered in the selected email or messaging application are handled by that application and its service provider under their own terms and privacy policies.

The player decides whether to share the invitation, which application to use and with whom the invitation is shared.

8. Purposes of processing

Information is processed only as reasonably necessary to:

  • Create, display and manage online rooms and connect hosts and guests.
  • Establish direct peer-to-peer communication or provide server-relayed communication when needed.
  • Synchronize controls, game objects, score and match state.
  • Deliver predefined phrase-chat messages.
  • Create and share optional invitation links for online rooms.
  • Restore temporarily interrupted multiplayer sessions.
  • Maintain security, prevent misuse and diagnose technical failures.

9. Legal basis

Where applicable under data-protection law, online session information is processed because it is necessary to provide the multiplayer functionality requested by the player. Limited technical and security information may also be processed for legitimate interests in operating, securing, debugging and protecting the service.

10. Direct and relayed connections

The game may use WebRTC or similar technology to establish a direct connection between participants. Network addresses and connection information may necessarily be exposed to other participants' devices as part of establishing a direct connection. If a direct connection cannot be established, gameplay data may be transmitted through the signaling service as a relay.

11. Hosting and service providers

Online services and this page may be hosted by third-party infrastructure providers, including Render. These providers may process IP addresses, timestamps, requests and operational logs as necessary to provide, secure and maintain their services. Google Play and Android may separately process information under Google's own terms and privacy policies.

12. Data sharing

The developer does not sell player information. Information may be shared only with participants in the same online room where needed for gameplay and phrase chat, with infrastructure providers that host or transmit the service, or when required by applicable law or necessary to protect users and the service.

13. Data retention

Online room membership, temporary connection identifiers, game state and phrase-chat messages are intended to exist only for the active session or a short technical reconnection period. Rooms and temporary session records are removed after the room closes, the relevant connection expires or the service restarts.

Infrastructure providers may retain technical server or security logs for limited operational periods determined by their configurations and policies.

14. Security

Reasonable technical measures are used, including HTTPS, secure WebSocket connections where supported, temporary connection identifiers and session-resume tokens. No Internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure, so players should not place sensitive or identifying information in player names or room names.

15. Permissions and device access

The game uses Internet access for optional multiplayer. It is not intended to access precise location, contacts, call logs, SMS messages, photos, microphone, camera, health data or advertising identifiers. If future versions introduce additional permissions or processing, this policy will be updated.

16. Advertising, analytics and purchases

The current version does not contain third-party advertising, behavioral advertising or in-app purchases. It does not intentionally use third-party analytics to create advertising or behavioral profiles.

17. Children's privacy

The game does not request a date of birth and does not require an account. Parents and guardians should supervise a minor's use of online multiplayer as appropriate. A parent or guardian who believes a child submitted personal information may contact the developer.

18. Player choices and requests

Players may avoid online processing by using offline mode and may remove locally stored information by clearing application storage or uninstalling the game. Because online session data is temporary and no account is required, the developer may not be able to identify a particular player's data after the session ends.

Subject to applicable law, players may contact the developer regarding access, correction, deletion, restriction or other data-protection rights.

19. International processing

Hosting and network providers may operate servers or process information outside the player's country. Where applicable, information is handled subject to provider safeguards, terms and applicable law.

20. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the game, multiplayer service, legal requirements or service providers change. The effective date at the top will be updated when a material revision is published.
