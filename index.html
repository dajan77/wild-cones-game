VR Controller Guide

Control scheme for VR controllers:

Left thumbstick Move player
Right thumbstick Rotate player
Left trigger Boost
Right trigger Lift stick
A button Start / Continue / Ready in online session
B button Change team strategy for AI player
X button Change camera
Y button Pause the game
Both thumbsticks, long press Soft recenter view
